    Frequently asked

    What happens when I place a call?

    Your browser's call goes into a private conference room on Vobiz first, then Vobiz dials the customer into that room. While their phone rings the panel shows Ringing, and it switches to On a call when they answer. If they never answer, the call ends on its own.

    Signed in with SIP direct, the panel cannot see the customer's leg, so it shows On a call as soon as Vobiz picks up your side — even while the customer's phone is still ringing.

    Why is the Call button disabled?

    Calling needs both a signed-in account and a live SIP registration. The hint under the button says which one is missing. Dialling without registration would connect the customer to silence.

    Who am I signed in as?

    Your own Freshdesk agent login decides which phone line you get, so colleagues on the same Vobiz account each have their own and never sign each other out. Sign out takes your line offline, and inbound calls stop ringing this browser.

    Do I need a headset?

    Audio runs through your computer's microphone and speakers. Your browser asks for microphone permission on the first call — allow it, or the other side hears nothing.

    Where do recordings go?

    Vobiz records the call and this panel lists recent ones above. Playback is proxied through your calling backend, because recording files need authentication a plain audio tag cannot send.
