Frequently asked

Why does the person I call hear ringing after answering?

Vobiz dials the customer first, then bridges your browser in. Those few seconds of ringback are that second leg connecting. It is expected, and the call order cannot be reversed.

Why is the Call button disabled?

Calling needs both a signed-in account and a live SIP registration. The hint under the button says which one is missing. Dialling without registration would connect the customer to silence.

Do I need a headset?

Audio runs through your computer's microphone and speakers. Your browser asks for microphone permission on the first call — allow it, or the other side hears nothing.

Where do recordings go?

Tick Record this call before dialling and Vobiz records that call. Leave it clear and nothing is recorded, and nothing is billed for recording.

Play them back in the Vobiz Console, under Voice → Recordings. They are not listed here: this panel would only be showing you a copy of what the Console already holds, and serving the audio through the calling backend meant that backend could hand out call recordings to anyone who could reach it.
